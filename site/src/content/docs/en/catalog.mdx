Each item has a **level**: *Safe* is fine for everyone, *Balanced* is for when you do not use the feature, and *Aggressive* is only for people who understand the trade-off. A **contested** item is a tweak experts disagree about. A **laptop-sensitive** item may cost battery or sleep on laptops. Some items **need a reboot**.

Open an item to see what it does, what may break and the command to undo it.
